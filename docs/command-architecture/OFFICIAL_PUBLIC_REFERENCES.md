# Official public references — Zambia Police Service national structure

Retrieved **28 November 2026** from the official Zambia Police Service website (zambiapolice.gov.zm) with a browser. These pages give the **published national organisational context**. They do not replace the internal Kamfinsa Mobile Unit structure confirmed by the project stakeholders (DEC-078 … DEC-080); the two are kept in their own contexts. Nothing here is used to invent internal Company, Detachment, Platoon or Area Commander relationships.

## Published facts and how the system reflects them

| Published fact (source) | In ZPS-OMS |
|---|---|
| The Inspector-General, subject to the President's direction, has overall command and control of the Force; may issue standing orders (Zambia Police Act, Cap. 107, s.3; R6). The IG office directs and coordinates the Service (R2). | IG office has no superior; no office can instruct it; DIG offices report to it (`command.ts`, Test A). |
| Service HQ comprises the Office of the IG; Office of the DIG – Administration (Administration, Research and Planning, Medical, Technical Services Directorates, Lilayi Police Training College); Office of the DIG – Operations (Crime Prevention and Support Services Directorate, National Command Centre); Office of the DIG – Special Duties/State House; CID; Legal and Professional Standards; Crime Intelligence Division; Finance; Internal Audit (R3). | DIG Operations, DIG Administration and DIG Special Duties / State House offices under the IG. DIG Administration has no Operations relationships and no access to Operations records pending H-3 (#6). CID, Crime Intelligence and investigations remain **out of scope** (CLAUDE.md exclusions). |
| The Office of the DIG – Operations coordinates Police operations and oversees the Provinces and: Crime Prevention and Support Services Directorate; Paramilitary Battalion; TAZARA Division; Kamfinsa School of Public Order Maintenance (SPOM)/Mobile Unit; Airport Division; Protective Unit; Police Air Wing; National Command Centre (R4). | All eight are organisation units (formations) under the national structure; the ten Provincial Divisions and the named formations are recorded as established coverage (DEC-078). |
| Kamfinsa SPOM/Mobile Unit: restores order in disturbed areas and provides public order training to ensure availability of mobile police officers for deployment (R4). | Administrative registry only (personnel, rosters, duty-area assignments, attendance status); no tactical deployment data. |
| A Division (province) is headed by a Divisional Commander (Commissioner of Police), assisted by a Deputy Divisional Commander (Deputy Commissioner of Police), a Divisional Operations Officer (Assistant Commissioner of Police) and other divisional officers; Districts are under Officers Commanding (R5). | Office types DIVISIONAL_COMMANDER and DEPUTY_DIVISION_COMMANDER carry the published ranks as information; Officer Commanding — District offices. The project's Staff I / Staff II operations offices are project requirements (DEC-074) and are kept. |
| Ten Divisions: Lusaka, Copperbelt, Central, Southern, Eastern, Northern, Western, Luapula, North-Western, Muchinga (R5). | Ten Provincial Division units (seed; DEC-078). |

## Not taken from public sources

The internal structure of Kamfinsa Mobile Unit — Companies, Detachments and duty stations, Company / Deputy / Detachment Commander offices, the Area Commander and Platoon Commander appointments and the Area Commander's two reporting channels — comes from the stakeholders' directive of 28 Nov 2026 and is not published on the website. Open questions about it are tracked in issues #2 and #3.

# DIG Administration — Portfolio Coverage Matrix

Final master directive, Part C (28 Nov 2026). v0.15.0. Status codes: **V** implemented and verified by automated tests · **P** partial · **O** outside the approved ZPS-OMS scope (recorded, not silently ignored) · **D** depends on an external input.

**Office and access.** The DIG Administration is an executive appointment reporting directly to the IG (`pos_dig_adm`). It has no application role among the 31 (DEC-072); its authority comes from the appointment (DEC-088): its own command and reporting with the IG, inter-office coordination, the training administration functions and its portfolio dashboard. Individual Operations records (personnel files, directives, reports, assignments) remain restricted pending the H-3 decision (issue #6); the dashboard shows workforce strength as aggregate counts only.

Published portfolio (official website, HQ page): Administration Directorate; Research and Planning Directorate; Medical Directorate; Technical Services Directorate; Lilayi Police Training College (`command-architecture/OFFICIAL_PUBLIC_REFERENCES.md`).

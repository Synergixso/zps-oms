## 1. How this plan treats institutional information

The directive separates two things, and so does this plan:

| | Meaning | Who settles it |
|---|---|---|
| **Institutional fact** | The structure, office, relationship or responsibility exists as stated. | The authorised ZPS stakeholders. **Facts supplied in the directive are confirmed and are not re-classified as hypothetical, provisional or unverified.** |
| **Implementation status** | Whether the software represents that fact correctly. | Technical audit and tests: fully implemented and verified, implemented not verified, partial, not implemented, blocked by a Service decision, blocked by an external dependency. |

Confirmed facts taken from the directive (§5–§13):

- The ten Provincial Divisions and the Divisional Units/formations (Kamfinsa Mobile Unit / SPOM, Paramilitary Battalion, Airport Division, TAZARA Division, Protective Unit and others authorised).
- The Inspector General of Police is the head of the Service and has no superior; the Deputy Inspectors General report to the IG and never instruct the IG.
- Instructions flow downward; acknowledgements, progress, completion and reports flow upward.
- Kamfinsa Mobile Unit Companies: **HQ Company, A Company, B Company, C Company, D Company**.
- Established Detachments and parent Companies: **HQ 5 Detachment (HQ Company, Kitwe, Copperbelt Province); B4 Detachment (B Company, Sikanze Police Camp, Lusaka); A2 Detachment (A Company, Kabwe, Central Province); A4 Detachment (A Company, Mansa, Luapula Province); D3 Detachment (D Company, Mufulira, Copperbelt Province); D4 Detachment (D Company, Choma, Southern Province).**
- Company Commander (Section Head / Officer-in-Charge of the Company) with a Deputy Company Commander who may assume duties under recorded, dated authority.
- Detachment Commander → Company Commander / Deputy Company Commander → Kamfinsa Mobile Unit HQ command structure.
- Area Commander (area-level coordination, liaison, representation, reporting) and Platoon Commander (**direct responsibility for the officers on the ground**) are distinct appointments.
- Field chain: Police Officer / Constable → Sergeant → Inspector → Chief Inspector / Platoon Commander → Area Commander. Rank and appointment are separate concepts.
- Area Commander parallel reporting: geographic channel (District Operations Officer, Officer Commanding) and Mobile Unit channel (Staff I / Staff II, Divisional Commander, Deputy Divisional Commander where delegated, then Commissioner/Deputy Commissioner Operations, DIG Operations, IG). One authoritative record; additional recipients, never duplicate assignments.

Constraints that remain in force (CLAUDE.md, DEC-028…DEC-077): administrative system only — no GPS/live location, patrol routes, tactical deployment or positions, dispatch, command and control, intelligence content, investigations or firearms records. A Detachment's **duty station** (e.g. "Kitwe, Copperbelt Province") is organisational metadata about where the unit is based, not a tracked position. Personnel, accounts and records in development remain synthetic; only the organisational structure supplied by the stakeholders is recorded as established.

## 4. Phases

Each phase ends with: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint`, `npm test`, the relevant Playwright journeys, `npm run audit:routes`, security probes where access changes, docs (CHANGELOG, decision register, status register, traceability), and a progress report (§8). A blocked item never stops unrelated work.

### Phase 1 — Audit and baseline *(this document)*
Skills verified; audit §3; plan committed; master issue and Project Manager issues opened.

### Phase 2 — Organisational hierarchy and command architecture
- **Database (migration 016):** `org_units.duty_station` (administrative base, nullable). Office types `COMPANY_COMMANDER`, `DEPUTY_COMPANY_COMMANDER`, `DETACHMENT_COMMANDER` (vocabulary in `command.ts`; proposed role correspondences: Company Commander → role 13, Detachment Commander → role 14 Section Head, recorded for validation, conferring nothing).
- **Established baseline (seed step, development; idempotent, additive):** HQ, A, B, C, D Companies under Kamfinsa Mobile Unit; the six Detachments under their stated Companies with their duty stations; established units carry `synthetic = 0` and the note "Established structure — Master Directive, Technical Services Section, Kamfinsa, 28 Nov 2026". The A/B Company records are relabelled in place (same ids, no data loss). The two placeholder detachments remain, clearly labelled as synthetic training units, until the Service supplies the full detachment list (issue).
- **Offices and relationships:** Company Commander and Deputy Company Commander for each Company; Detachment Commander for each Detachment. Detachment Commander → Company Commander (primary command) and → Deputy Company Commander (parallel reporting, so the deputy receives reports); Deputy Company Commander → Company Commander; Company Commander → Divisional Commander, Mobile Unit (primary command — the exact KMU HQ superior office is asked of the Project Manager; relationships are configuration and can be changed by `command.structure.manage` without code). The Deputy assumes the Company Commander's duties through an acting appointment (authority, delegated actions, effective dates, duration — existing C-10).
- **Labels:** Provincial Divisions and formations recorded as established institutional coverage (not "provisional").
- **Tests:** structure (five Companies, six Detachments under the stated parents, duty stations), offices and relationships, Detachment report reaching Company Commander (primary) and Deputy (parallel), Deputy acting as Company Commander within dates only, no Detachment Commander visibility into another Company.

### Phase 3 — Operational instructions and task management
Directives, acknowledgement, progress, forwarding, completion, conflicts and upward reports already exist (A7). Remaining: the §13.4 accountability panel on directive and report pages (originating officer, issuing authority, responsible officer = current holder of the receiving office, parent unit, recipients, acknowledgement, progress, completion, escalation history, audit trail) — server-side and within existing visibility rules; tests.

### Phase 4 — Kamfinsa Mobile Unit operational management
- **Platoon Commander workspace** (`/app/command/platoon`): for holders (substantive or acting) of a Platoon Commander office — subordinate offices and holders down the field chain; the platoon's personnel and availability (only where the holder's personnel scope covers the platoon); open instructions to subordinate offices with acknowledgement and progress; reports received; **daily attendance (muster) register**: one muster per platoon per day, an administrative status per officer (present, on leave, sick, off duty, on course, absent), recorded by the Platoon Commander office only (new delegable action *Record platoon attendance*), audited, no location or free-text fields.
- **Area Commander workspace** (`/app/command/area`): platoons under the area command with attendance **counts** (no individual personnel management — distinct from the Platoon Commander), reports from Platoon Commanders awaiting acknowledgement, the two reporting channels and their recipients, instructions issued, and a **partner-organisation engagement register** (e.g. KCM, Mopani Mine management, security coordination meetings): date, partner, engagement type, attending office, reference, follow-up status — metadata only, no minutes or content.
- **Company / Detachment Commander workspace** (`/app/command/company`): Detachments, duty stations, commanders, personnel counts, reports from Detachment Commanders, instructions.
- **Tests:** A–H style domain tests for musters (office required, acting action required, platoon membership, one per day, area sees counts only, cross-platoon refused), engagements, workspaces; security probes for cross-unit IDs.

Deny by default; authority from offices (never rank or role alone); data access additionally bounded by the actor's personnel scope; tenant isolation server-side; `loadOr404` for out-of-scope ids; liaison (roles 07, 19, 30, 31) and technical accounts hold no office; every mutation audited in the hash chain; no content field on liaison or engagement records; excluded-term screening on free text.

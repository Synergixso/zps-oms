# Prompt 01 — Institutional Discovery Workshop Plan

Prepare a structured discovery plan for authorised Zambia Police Service stakeholders.

Use the stakeholder groups and interview guide in the Phase 1 tracker. For each workshop, specify purpose, roles (not personal names), duration, pre-reading, evidence to request, questions, process-walkthrough steps, risks and output template.

Focus on actual administrative workflows within DIG Ops HQ, ten Provincial Divisions and authorised DIG Ops formations. Examples to validate include organisational register changes, generic administrative requests/approvals, general asset accountability and vehicle maintenance references. Do not presume they are current pain points. Technical Services has publicly documented ICT and mechanical/vehicle maintenance responsibilities; explicitly validate current system-of-record ownership before proposing duplicate features.

Do not seek actual rosters, deployment details, live service records, personal files or operational registers. Use blank templates, redacted samples approved by the Service, synthetic scenarios and process-owner demonstrations. Do not record sensitive information in minutes.

Outputs: workshop calendar proposal (no cost assumed, subject to availability), stakeholder question sets, process observation checklists, evidence register, action log and list of unresolved decisions. Do not claim workshops occurred.

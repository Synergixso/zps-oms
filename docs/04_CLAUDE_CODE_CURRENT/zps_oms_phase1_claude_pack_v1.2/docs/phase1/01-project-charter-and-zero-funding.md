# 01 — Project Charter and Zero-Funding Position

## Purpose

Prepare and validate requirements for a proposed administrative Operations Management System supporting the Zambia Police Service operations structure from Service Headquarters under DIG Ops through the Provincial Divisions and authorised DIG Ops formations.

## Sponsor and authority (proposed)

- Institutional authority: Zambia Police Service High Command.
- Proposed business sponsor: Deputy Inspector General – Operations, subject to formal appointment/confirmation.
- Technical stakeholder: Technical Services Directorate, subject to formal technical governance confirmation.
- Functional stakeholders: authorised representatives from provincial divisions and DIG Ops formations.

These are proposed roles, not institutional appointments or approvals.

## Current-stage deliverable

Complete documentation, institutional discovery instruments, requirements validation, UX exploration and (only if continued and toolchain permits) a local prototype using synthetic data.

## Funding rule

No project funding is requested or required for the current documentation and local synthetic-data prototype stage. The approach uses existing permitted tools and in-kind effort. No procurement, paid hosting, subscriptions, external APIs, hardware, travel, allowances or purchases are authorised.

If any necessary action requires money, stop; record it as a future-stage open decision. Future production hosting, security assessment, support, devices, training, rollout and maintenance are not covered by this zero-cash position and require a separate assessment and approval.

## Benefits to validate

Potential benefits include improved administrative workflow traceability, better quality of authorised registers, faster retrieval of approved records and improved management summaries. No current service pain point or baseline benefit is confirmed until internal discovery provides evidence.

## Governance

High Command provides institutional authority; DIG Ops is proposed business sponsor; Technical Services is the principal technical stakeholder; module/process owners are nominated by the Service; Legal/Privacy/Security and records/audit functions review relevant controls.

## Current status

Documentation pack prepared for review. Internal interviews, process observation, official hierarchy confirmation, data classification, technical toolchain review and formal Phase 1 sign-off remain outstanding.

# 04 — Governance, RACI and Decision Control

## Proposed roles

- High Command: institutional authority and scope approval.
- DIG Ops: proposed business sponsor and owner of the Operations product boundary.
- Technical Services Directorate: technical architecture, toolchain, infrastructure and system-support review.
- Business process owners: nominated owners for each workflow/data domain.
- Legal/Privacy/Records/Security/Audit: relevant control and compliance reviews.
- Developer: documentation, design and synthetic prototype work under approved direction.
- Procurement/Finance: only involved if a later stage proposes a procurement or public investment decision.

The workbook contains a proposed RACI matrix. It does not establish authority. Each role and delegation must be confirmed by the Service.

## Critical open decisions

- Current DIG Ops establishment, titles and reporting links.
- Official names and status of the ten Provincial Divisions and authorised formations.
- Whether intermediate positions are represented as system nodes or user roles.
- Business and data owner for every candidate module.
- Existing systems of record and any duplicate workflow.
- Delegated approval matrix and records retention.
- Field-level data classification and lawful purpose.
- Claude Code/AI tooling, repository and package/tool policy.
- Local prototype and future test/pilot authorisation.
- Source-code ownership, use rights and exit arrangements.
- Confirmation of zero-cash rule and stop conditions.

All decisions remain open until approved evidence is recorded in the tracker.

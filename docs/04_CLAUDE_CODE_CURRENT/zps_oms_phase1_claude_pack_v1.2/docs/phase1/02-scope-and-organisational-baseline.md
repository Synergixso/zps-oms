# 02 — Scope and Organisational Baseline

## In-scope organisational span

1. Zambia Police Service Headquarters under the Office of the Deputy Inspector General – Operations (DIG Ops).
2. Ten Provincial Police Divisions: Lusaka, Copperbelt, Central, Southern, Eastern, Northern, Luapula, Western, North-Western and Muchinga.
3. Authorised DIG Ops Divisional Units/formations as confirmed by the current approved internal establishment.

The public DIG Ops webpage identifies oversight of Provinces and lists Crime Prevention and Support Services Directorate, Paramilitary Battalion, TAZARA Division, Kamfinsa SPOM/Mobile Unit, Airport Division, Protective Unit, Police Air Wing and National Command Centre. The published Appendix D organogram shows a Deputy Police Commander (PSS01), Commissioner of Police (PSS02), and eight PSS03 subordinate positions. Public page and chart naming differs from the user's terminology and must be reconciled against the current signed internal establishment.

## Explicit exclusions

- Districts, police stations and police posts as system hierarchy or tenant levels.
- Police training administration and the separate national Training Management System.
- General HR, payroll, medical and biometric systems.
- Tactical command, dispatch, operational tasking/deployment plans, patrol routes, live tracking, intelligence, informants, surveillance, investigations, case files, evidence/exhibits, firearms/armoury and sensitive capability/vulnerability information.

## Organisational data model proposal

Use a configurable and effective-dated directory. Each node should have a stable internal identifier, approved official name, entity type, parent reference, status, effective-from/to dates, approval reference and change history. Do not seed real officer accounts or real records. Do not assume all formations report directly to DIG Ops. Do not import the public organogram as production configuration.

## Validation precedence

Current approved internal establishment and signed instructions prevail over the public website, organogram, earlier documents, user shorthand and all developer assumptions.

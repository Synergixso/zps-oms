# 05 — Data, Security and Legal Review Requirements

## Current data posture

Public-source and synthetic data only. No live police information or internal system connection. The data inventory in the tracker is a candidate list, not an approved data-processing specification.

## Minimum safeguards for any future approved environment

- Document the purpose and lawful basis for each personal-data processing activity.
- Determine controller/processor roles and applicable registrations/assessments.
- Minimise collection and restrict free-text fields.
- Establish information classification, retention, archive and disposal rules.
- Use approved identity management and MFA where required.
- Enforce least privilege and organisation-scoped server-side authorisation.
- Separate technical administration from business-data permissions.
- Maintain protected audit logs for access, changes, approvals and exports.
- Protect data in transit and at rest using approved government standards.
- Establish secure backups, recovery procedures and incident response.
- Review dependencies, secrets, telemetry, external AI tools and repository access.

## Legal issue-spotting references

- Data Protection Act, 2021 (Act 3 of 2021). ZambiaLII records commencement on 1 April 2021.
- Cyber Security Act, 2025 (Act 3 of 2025). ZambiaLII records commencement on 12 May 2025; critical information/infrastructure implications require legal and competent-authority assessment.
- Cyber Crimes Act, 2025 (Act 4 of 2025). ZambiaLII records commencement on 12 May 2025.
- Electronic Government Act, 2021 (Act 41 of 2021): confirm the relevant commencement instrument and applicable government ICT requirements.
- Public Procurement Act, 2020 and 2023 amendment: relevant only if future procurement is proposed; no procurement is requested at this stage.
- SMART Zambia Institute guidance: ICT systems acquisition, cloud services, legacy replacement, cybersecurity, service-level agreements, business continuity/disaster recovery, electronic records and data interoperability.

These references are not a legal opinion. Legal counsel and the competent government authorities must confirm current consolidated legislation, regulations, applicability, exemptions, data-controller status, critical-infrastructure designation and operational requirements before any real data or production system is used.

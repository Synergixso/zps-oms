# 03 — Candidate Requirements and Process Validation

## Candidate process families

1. Organisation and formation directory maintenance.
2. National and provincial administrative summary reporting.
3. Non-sensitive internal administrative requests and approvals.
4. General asset accountability, subject to asset owner approval.
5. Fleet administrative references and maintenance request status, only after source-of-record and Technical Services ownership are confirmed.
6. Internal document/correspondence routing only if an authorised process owner confirms the scope and classification.
7. Technical service requests only if they do not duplicate the current Technical Services service desk.
8. Controlled reporting and exports.

## Candidate requirements handling

The tracker includes 43 draft requirements. They must be reviewed with nominated process owners and dispositioned as Accept, Revise, Defer or Reject. Every accepted requirement must have:
- a named business owner;
- evidence of the problem or obligation;
- a lawful and specific purpose where personal data is involved;
- data fields and classification;
- an approved workflow and delegation;
- role and organisation-scope rules;
- acceptance criteria;
- dependencies and exclusion checks.

## Discovery method

Conduct role-based interviews and observe authorised administrative processes using blank forms, approved redacted templates or non-sensitive demonstrations. Do not request actual rosters, live personnel files, operational logs or classified examples. Record confirmed facts separately from hypotheses.

## Candidate MVP selection

Do not pick an MVP solely because it is easy to code. Compare process volume, error/delay evidence, owner readiness, data sensitivity, existing system overlap, security, ability to measure improvement and zero-cash feasibility. The initial synthetic prototype may test organisation-directory configuration and one generic administrative-request workflow without real police records.

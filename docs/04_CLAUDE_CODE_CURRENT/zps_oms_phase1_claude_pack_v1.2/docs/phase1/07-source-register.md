The current ZambiaLII legal records state that the Data Protection Act commenced on 1 April 2021 and both 2025 cyber Acts commenced on 12 May 2025. Legal advisers should verify the current consolidated texts, later amendments, regulations, sector-specific duties, critical-infrastructure designation, controller/processor roles and applicable government ICT standards. Confirm the Electronic Government Act commencement instrument. Procurement law is relevant if a later stage proposes procurement; no procurement is requested for the present stage.

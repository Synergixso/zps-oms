# Scope and organisational boundary

Apply the project scope in `CLAUDE.md` without exception. Only DIG Ops Headquarters, the ten Provincial Divisions and authorised DIG Ops Divisional Units/formations are in scope. No districts, police stations or posts. The separate Training Management System is outside ZPS-OMS.

Maintain a source-of-truth distinction: current approved internal establishment > signed internal directives > official public ZPS pages and organograms > developer assumptions. Public sources never confer authority to create a user role, reporting relationship or data field.

Do not hard-code public chart labels. Use effective-dated, configurable organisation entities. Any change to scope or hierarchy must be recorded as a decision and approved by the nominated authority.
